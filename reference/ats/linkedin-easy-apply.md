# LinkedIn Easy Apply

**Not automated, in any LinkedIn mode.** Easy Apply is an action on linkedin.com, and LinkedIn does not permit browser extensions that automate activity on its site; accounts that use them risk restriction (see `reference/sources/linkedin.md`). Seekter lists Easy Apply postings in the report under "On LinkedIn, yours to send" and logs them as `pending`. It does not open the modal, pick a CV, answer questions or submit.

The driver and the measurements that used to live here (translated button labels, single-page modals, `role=radio` screening questions, the pre-ticked follow box) were removed in the release that stopped Easy Apply automation. They are in git history. Do not restore them.
